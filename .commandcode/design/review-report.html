P0 · HIGH · COLOR

Faint-text token fails WCAG everywhere it is used

--color-text-faint = neutral-600 (#a49c8c light / #6a6459 dark), consumed by .card-meta at 11px, the select placeholder, and four resume classes at 0.72–0.88em. Measured 2.43:1 to 3.35:1 across both modes — below AA at exactly the sizes least able to survive it.

FIX: Re-point consumers at --color-text-muted (5.59:1 / 6.39:1, already passing) or lift the faint step to ≥4.5:1 on both grounds. One token change repairs every card meta, caption row, and resume datum. Run /design recolor.
